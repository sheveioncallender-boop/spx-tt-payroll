Trinidad & Tobago Payroll

Modern Trinidad & Tobago payroll built directly into Odoo

Complete attendance-first payroll management for Trinidad & Tobago, integrated with Odoo Employees, Attendances, Work Schedules, and Time Off.

Simple payroll processing. Complete Trinidad statutory control.

Trinidad & Tobago Payroll provides a complete, easy-to-use payroll workspace for companies operating in Trinidad and Tobago. It works directly with Odoo Employees, Attendances, Work Schedules, and Time Off. The module automatically pulls employee attendance and approved leave into payroll runs, calculates payable hours, prorates salaries, processes recurring additions and deductions, and generates professional employee payslips.

Automatic statutory calculations include PAYE, Employee NIS, Employer NIS, and Health Surcharge. Payroll officers can review employee calculations, resolve attendance issues, apply controlled payable-hours adjustments with required reasons, and monitor payroll totals through a modern interactive dashboard.

Key Features

Employee & Attendance Integration

Uses existing Odoo Employees, Attendances, Work Schedules, and Time Off records without creating duplicate employee or leave systems.

Flexible Payroll Processing

Supports salaried and hourly-paid employees with weekly, fortnightly, semi-monthly, monthly, and off-cycle payroll runs.

Attendance-First Salary Calculation

Automatically loads eligible employees, pulls attendance and approved leave, calculates payable hours, and prorates salary where required.

Trinidad Statutory Calculations

Calculates PAYE, Employee NIS, Employer NIS, and Health Surcharge, with employee-specific statutory settings and year-to-date tracking.

Earnings, Deductions & Loans

Processes recurring allowances, additions, deductions, and loans with taxable earnings included by default and a simple Exclude from PAYE option.

Guided Review & Audit History

Guided attention items take users directly to issues. Manual payable-hours adjustments require reasons and preserve permanent before-and-after history.

Professional Payslips

Generates modern US Letter portrait payslips with Wages, Bank Transfers, Additions, Taxes, Deductions, Loans, and Current/YTD summaries.

Modern Payroll Dashboard

Live cards provide payroll progress, alerts, employees, attendance, statutory liabilities, payroll cost, payments, payslips, and recent payroll runs.

Designed for practical use

The module is designed to keep payroll straightforward for everyday users while providing the controls, statutory information, multi-company support, and detailed records required by payroll officers and management. It works with Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise and remains independent from Accounting and the Trinidad accounting localization.
