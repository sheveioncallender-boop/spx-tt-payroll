# TT Payroll 19.0.1.8.6 — test build

Developed by Spxcorp Limited. Based on v1.8.5, with Phase 6 kept on a separate branch.

## Native Odoo workflow

1. Employees clock in and out in Odoo Attendances. Employee records, work schedules, overtime decisions and Time Off approval stay in the standard Odoo apps.
2. Generate a TT Payroll run for the company, frequency and dates. Eligible employees are loaded once, with their attendance and approved leave snapshots.
3. Correct incorrect clockings in Odoo Attendances, then use Refresh Attendance & Time Off in payroll. This deliberately clears payroll attendance confirmation.
4. If an authorised payroll-only correction is needed, use Adjust Payable Hours and enter a reason. This records before/after hours and pay, the user and time. It never writes a clock-in or check-out.
5. Confirm attendance, calculate pay and review the employee calculations. A confirmed short period may be accepted with an Attendance Review Note; it is not necessary to inflate hours to clear a shortage.
6. Use the existing approval/payment actions. Approved and paid calculations and payslip summaries are protected. Accounting remains optional; no accounting entries or bank payments are generated by this build.

## Corrections

- Annual PAYE projection accounts for approved/paid history and opening YTD amounts. Monthly projection uses remaining months; weekly projection uses remaining Mondays when history exists, otherwise the year's Mondays. Semi-monthly and fortnightly follow corresponding period equivalents; they are not direct TT Paysheet calculator benchmarks.
- NIS relief projects weekly employee contributions across the applicable Mondays. Only confirmed, effective TD1 adjustments are used, with the existing combined pension/NIS relief cap and annual tax bands.
- One-off Payment on an earning counts the amount once in the tax projection. Ordinary additions remain taxable by default; Exclude from PAYE still leaves gross pay unchanged.
- NIS equivalent earnings are rounded to currency precision before class lookup, closing the fractional-cent gap for fortnightly earnings.
- Manual addition/deduction changes refresh statutory calculations and invalidate employee review. Percentage recurring items follow the recalculated wage base.
- Negative overtime totals cannot create extra normal worked hours. Attendance crossing period boundaries uses Odoo worked hours in each portion to allocate overtime, without changing Odoo records.
- Direct status writes, forged boolean contexts, new items on locked payrolls, and direct overwrites of payroll hours/totals are blocked. The existing hours wizard remains the adjustment route.
- Payroll changes have immutable history. Completed runs cannot be reset or cancelled; the manager reopening and adjustment-payroll features remain Phase 6/future work.
- Statutory YTD excludes held/excluded historical lines. Completed payslip summaries and identifying labels are frozen.
- Odoo 19 constraint declarations replace the unsupported legacy declarations. Upgrade checks stop on duplicates rather than deleting or silently merging them.

## Verification and limits

Local verification: Python compilation, manifest file checks, XML and Odoo 19 XML/list schemas, actual source method arithmetic with ORM boundary doubles, source wiring/guard checks, and 12 independent projection tests. The full case results are in checks/results.json. The doubles do not execute database constraints, access rules, field recomputation, transactions or rendered screens.

The new Odoo TransactionCase scenarios cover native clockings, checkout correction/refresh, payroll-only adjustment, live deduction recalculation, TD1 validity, existing employee loading, locked history and short-pay review. Those require a real Odoo database. GitHub Actions is configured to install/test the addon on Odoo 19 Community; consult the specific commit's Actions result. Enterprise testing will be performed on the user's Cloudpepper test instance. Enterprise source is not included or required in this addon.

Do not treat this build as a production sign-off. On Enterprise verify fresh install/upgrade, each payroll role, company isolation, native attendance and leave integration, payslip PDF layout, concurrent changes and the complete calculate/review/approve/paid flow.

## Important limitations

- Off-cycle runs remain available for preparation but cannot be approved in this build. Contribution allocation and incremental PAYE for overlapping off-cycle payments still need implementation/validation; repeating the normal period allowance is unsafe.
- NIS tables before 5 January 2026 are not included and prevent approval. Future rate changes require a table update; this build does not download rates automatically.
- With no earlier monthly pay recorded, the projection assumes no earlier taxable earnings. Enter opening YTD amounts before processing an employee who was already paid earlier in the year. Changing opening values affects open payroll; it does not rewrite completed payslips.
- Semi-monthly/fortnightly dates, partial weeks, joiners/leavers, public-holiday work and unusual overtime must be reconciled against real payroll examples during acceptance testing. Existing automatic overtime still uses the established 1.5 multiplier; a full overtime rules phase is not included.
- Frozen legacy payslips capture their values at upgrade; the module cannot reconstruct history that was changed before this protection existed.
- The new safeguards prevent direct changes to completed payroll; an approved-but-unpaid reopening wizard is not included while Phase 6 is paused.

## Cloudpepper test installation

Use the repository's main branch after the v1.8.6 commit is published. Back up the test database before upgrading. Update the repository/rebuild in Cloudpepper, then upgrade Trinidad & Tobago Payroll in Apps (do not uninstall). Confirm version 19.0.1.8.6. Recalculate and re-review existing unfinished payrolls. Completed financial amounts are not recalculated during upgrade.

The supported upgrade target is v1.8.5. Older migration chains need separate testing before use. If duplicate records are detected, the upgrade stops with their IDs; resolve them on a test copy rather than deleting payroll history blindly.
